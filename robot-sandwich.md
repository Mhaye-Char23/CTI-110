
# Simple PBJ Sandwich

## Assumptions
- Bread in the pantry 
- clean workspace countertop with a napkin ontop 
- Peanut butter in pantry 
- Jelly is in a cabinet under the countertop of the workstation
- The robot should be getting ready to get needed items out
- Butter knife is out on the countertop

## Instructions
1. Robot turns around and opens the pantry
2. Robot identifies where the bread is and grabs it
3. Robot identifies where the peanut butter is and grabs it
4. Robot closes the pantry and turns back around to the countertop\
5. Robot sets the items in hands on the countertop
6. Robot goes around the countertop and opens the cabinet thats under the countertop
7. Robot searches for the jelly
8. Robot grabs the jelly when found
9. Robot closes the cabinet and goes back around the countertop to the starting spot
10. Robot puts down jelly
11. Robot opens bread bag 
12. Robot takes out two slices of bread 
13. Robot puts the two slices of bread on the napkin on the countertop
14. Robot closes the bread bag and puts it to the side
15. Robot opens the peanut butter 
16. Robot grabs butter knife and puts some peanut butter on the butter knife
17. Robot spreads the peanut butter on one slice of bread evenly
18. Robot opens the jar of jelly 
19. Robot takes the same butter knife and puts jelly on it
20. Robot spreads the jelly on the other untouched slice of bread evenly
21. Robot places the butter knife down on the napkin
22. Robot puts one hand under the slice of peanut butter bread
23. Robot slides the other hand under the slice of jelly bread
24. Robot puts the jelly slice of bread ontop of the Peanut butter slice of bread

## Done when
- Robot puts the jelly slice of bread ontop of the peanut butter slice of bread
